## Context

Alongside project-based engagements, a recurring part of the role is managed security services (MSSP) work: ongoing operational responsibility for multiple clients' security platforms, covering both planned changes and incident response — the day-to-day of keeping production security infrastructure running correctly, not just standing it up once.

## What I do

- Manage Akamai across its full stack for client environments: CDN configuration, WAF policy, DNS, and Zero Trust access
- Operate F5 Distributed Cloud and F5 BIG-IP platforms for clients running application delivery and security through F5
- Manage firewall platforms, Cisco, Palo Alto Networks and Azure WAF, handling policy changes and operational issues both via GUI and Infrastructure as Code (IaC)
- Manage cloud-native WAF (Azure WAF, GCP Cloud Armor), policy changes, exclusion and respond to incident
- Handle change requests through structured change management processes (ServiceNow and Jira), and respond to incidents as they arise across this platform portfolio
- Perform troubleshooting across all of the above when something isn't behaving as expected, often under time pressure during active incidents

## Why it matters

Building something once is a different skill from operating it reliably over time. This is the side of the job that involves triaging an incident on a platform I didn't originally configure, working within a client's existing change process instead of designing my own from scratch, and keeping several different vendors' platforms running correctly in parallel.
